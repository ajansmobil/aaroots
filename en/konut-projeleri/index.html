Residential Projects

A.A. ROOTS delivers a wide range of residential projects from multi-storey buildings and housing estates to boutique living and socially equipped homes.

We apply reliable project management, on-time delivery and quality workmanship to every project. All stages are shared with you through visuals, reports and site meetings. From Bodrum across Turkey we build residential spaces that raise living standards, with environmentally sound and sustainable solutions.

We offer turnkey solutions for estates including infrastructure, common areas and landscaping. For boutique projects we design smaller, manageable living environments. We follow TS EN ISO standards, experienced project management and certified materials; our homes are earthquake-compliant and energy efficient.

Our team supports you through planning, permits and cost management. Our aim is aesthetic, durable and long-lasting living spaces and full customer satisfaction. Contact A.A. ROOTS for details and quotes on residential projects.
