Commercial Buildings

A.A. ROOTS builds office buildings, retail units, hotels and mixed-use commercial projects, delivering functional and durable spaces tailored to business needs.

We ensure full compliance with fire safety, accessibility and energy efficiency standards. With project-based solutions we can plan office floors, retail areas, hotel rooms and common spaces under one roof. All materials are top grade and certified; we keep to every agreed delivery date.

We have strong experience in tourism and commercial projects in Bodrum and the Aegean. In hotel and apart-hotel construction we combine comfort, aesthetics and longevity; in retail and showroom projects we add value to your brand through façade and interior design. Every Roots building adds value to its location.

You can use our survey, cost analysis and project management services for your commercial project. We share all stages through transparent process management, visuals, reports and site meetings. Realise your commercial project with A.A. ROOTS with confidence.
