Renovation & Refurbishment

A.A. ROOTS provides professional renovation and refurbishment services to renew, strengthen and modernise existing buildings. Our expert team supports you in improving both the look and function of older properties.

For interior refurbishment we carry out kitchen, bathroom, floor and ceiling upgrades; for exterior work we do insulation, painting and façade improvements with quality materials and workmanship. Seismic strengthening and structural improvements are also part of our scope. We prefer the highest quality certified materials; all brands are shared in detail at the survey stage.

We are your single point of contact for renewing Bodrum holiday and permanent homes and for refurbishing hotels and businesses. We plan from minimal intervention to full renovation according to your needs. We work in full compliance with health and safety rules (H&S).

All our material choices are quality and certified. Renovation costs vary by project scope. Contact A.A. ROOTS for survey and free assessment.
