Survey & Project Consulting

A.A. ROOTS offers pre-construction survey, cost analysis and project consulting as part of our commitment to thorough survey, planning and controls. After survey we provide a detailed delivery schedule; we are with you from technical assessment of your site or existing building through to budget and programme planning.

Our survey covers soil conditions, zoning, infrastructure and feasibility. In project consulting we guide you on review of architectural and structural designs, material selection, permit processes and programme preparation. With our experienced project and site team we run client communication and feedback at every stage.

With cost analysis we produce rough and detailed project costs and help you plan a transparent budget in line with market conditions. Transparent communication is a priority; we keep you informed through regular reporting, visuals and site follow-up. We operate mainly in Bodrum and the surrounding area and can also deliver projects in other regions of Turkey.

Survey and consulting can be one-off or ongoing for the project duration. Contact A.A. ROOTS experts to start your dream project on the right footing.
