Villa Construction

A.A. ROOTS specialises in custom villa construction across Bodrum and Turkey. We deliver fully bespoke villa projects tailored to your needs, style and plot, combining modern architecture, durable materials and turnkey delivery.

We work alongside you from site selection and design through to foundations and interior-exterior finishing. We build villas suited to Bodrum's climate and local building standards, fully compliant with seismic codes and energy efficiency. Our team is experienced and site-focused.

All materials we use are top grade and certified; we deliver long-lasting, value-holding structures. We keep to every agreed delivery date. Whether you imagine a single-storey garden villa or a multi-level sea-view home, we shape your project with you. Every Roots building adds value to its location.

All project stages are shared through visuals, reports and site meetings. Villa construction costs depend on location, size and materials. Contact A.A. ROOTS for survey and free consultancy. Build your dream villa with us on a solid foundation, strong future basis.
